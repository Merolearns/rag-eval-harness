What it measures
  • Recall@k: did the relevant document appear in the top-k results?
  • Precision@k: how many of the top-k documents are relevant?
  • MRR: mean reciprocal rank of the first relevant document.
  • Token-level answer F1 and exact-match rate against the ground-truth answer.
  • Latency per question measured on the local evaluator.
